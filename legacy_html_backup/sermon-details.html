Overcoming Evil with Love and Peace

Rt. Rev. Bishop Abraham Matiop Deng May 24, 2026 Romans 12:9-21

"Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good." — Romans 12:21

Pastoral Message Outline

In this inspiring sermon delivered at the Diocesan Cathedral in Jalle, Bishop Abraham Matiop reflects on the apostle Paul's injunction to the church in Rome, providing practical wisdom for our community in South Sudan:

1. Love Must Be Sincere (v. 9)

Genuine Christian love rejects hypocrisy. It mourns with those who mourn, rejoices with those who rejoice, and actively seeks the welfare of our neighbors.

2. Faithful in Prayer, Patient in Affliction (v. 12)

South Sudan has faced many seasons of hardship, but as children of God, our anchor is in Christ. We are commanded to remain constant in prayer and patient under trials.

3. Living in Harmony with All (v. 18)

'If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone.' Peacemaking is not optional for followers of Christ; it is a sacred mandate.

Prayer for the Diocese of Jalle

"Almighty and everlasting God, who has given us grace in this time of fellowship to make our common supplications unto Thee: Pour Thy Holy Spirit upon the people of Jalle, our families, leaders, and children. Grant us hearts of peace, hands of mercy, and voices of truth, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen."
